# Repayment, title & default

## Repayment

Every vault loan is a fixed-rate, fully amortizing loan:

- **Rate:** 10% fixed APR for the full term.
- **Term:** 36 months.
- **Payments:** equal monthly payments of principal and interest.

Payments are paid out to the vault's lenders in proportion to their share of the lender side.

## Title

Zipcode holds the hardware in custody throughout the term. When the final payment clears, **title transfers to the borrower**.

## Missed payments and default

The hardware is the collateral for the loan. If a borrower misses payments, the hardware is sold and the proceeds are used to make lenders whole.

Because the borrower funds 50% of the purchase price up front, the amount owed to lenders starts at half the hardware's purchase price and falls with every payment.
