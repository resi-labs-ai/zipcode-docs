# For borrowers

A Zipcode vault lets you acquire datacenter- or workstation-class GPUs by funding half the purchase price. Zipcode handles procurement, hosting and maintenance. You operate the hardware and repay over 36 months.

## Who it is for

- **Miners and network operators** who want more capacity on networks such as Instant, Chutes, Engy, Lium and Targon.
- **AI teams** that need dedicated GPUs for training or inference without paying the full price up front.
- **Compute providers** growing a fleet one purchase at a time.

## What you need

- A deposit of **50% of the purchase price** for the hardware you choose.
- The ability to meet **36 equal monthly payments** at a **10% fixed APR**.

There is no credit application and no credit score. Your deposit, and the fact that the hardware stays in Zipcode custody until it is paid off, underwrite the loan.

## How it works for you

1. **Choose your hardware** and open a vault for that configuration.
2. **Deposit your 50%.** Your vault then opens to lenders for the other half.
3. **Zipcode procures and installs** the hardware in a verified colocation facility once the vault is fully funded.
4. **Receive SSH access.** You control what runs on the machine; Zipcode manages the physical hardware, power, cooling and connectivity.
5. **Put it to work.** Deploy on a supported network to earn. See [Earning on Networks](/vaults/earning).
6. **Repay monthly.** When the final payment clears, title transfers to you.

## What you are responsible for

- Making each monthly payment in full and on time.
- The workloads you run and how you configure the software on the machine.
- Complying with the rules of any network you deploy on.

## What Zipcode is responsible for

- Sourcing and purchasing the hardware.
- Hosting it in a verified colocation facility.
- Physical management and maintenance for the life of the loan.
- Servicing the loan and distributing payments to lenders.
