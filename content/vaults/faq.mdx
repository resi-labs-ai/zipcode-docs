# FAQ

### What is a vault?

A vault is a single hardware purchase, financed 50% by the borrower and 50% by lenders at a 10% fixed APR over 36 months.

### Do I need a credit check to borrow?

No. The borrower's 50% deposit and Zipcode's custody of the hardware underwrite the loan.

### Where is the hardware?

In a verified colocation facility, where Zipcode manages and maintains it for the life of the loan. See [Hardware & Operations](/vaults/operations).

### Can I use the hardware before it is paid off?

Yes. Borrowers receive secure SSH access and can deploy the hardware on supported networks, including Instant, Chutes, Engy, Lium and Targon. See [Earning on Networks](/vaults/earning).

### When do I own the hardware?

Title transfers to the borrower when the final payment clears.

### What do lenders earn?

A 10% fixed APR, paid from the borrower's monthly payments.

### What happens if a borrower stops paying?

The hardware is sold to make lenders whole. See [Repayment, Title & Default](/vaults/repayment).

### Are the vaults on the site live?

Not yet. Vaults and figures shown are illustrative until launch. Join the waitlist at [zipcode.finance](/) to be notified.
