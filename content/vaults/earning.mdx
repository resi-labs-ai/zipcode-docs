# Earning on networks

Financed hardware can earn while the loan is being repaid. With SSH access to their system, borrowers can deploy it on supported compute networks:

- **Instant**
- **Chutes**
- **Engy**
- **Lium**
- **Targon**

## How it works

1. Your hardware is installed and running in a verified colocation facility.
2. You connect over SSH and configure your environment.
3. You register and run on the network or networks of your choice, under each network's own rules.
4. Earnings accrue to you as the operator.

## Earnings and your loan

<Callout type="warning">
  Network earnings are variable and are not guaranteed. They depend on each network's demand, pricing and rules, and
  on how the hardware is configured and operated.
</Callout>

Your loan payments are fixed and are due every month regardless of what the hardware earns. You may choose to put network earnings toward your payments, but the obligation to repay is yours either way.
